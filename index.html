FAQ

Straight answers.

What's a fractional CMO, exactly?

Senior marketing leadership on a part-time basis. You get the strategy, accountability and team leadership of a CMO for a fraction of the cost and commitment of a full-time executive hire.

Do we have to start with the Traction Audit?

It's the recommended starting point, because it tells both of us exactly where the leverage is. If you already have a solid diagnosis, we can review it and go straight into a Growth Engine Build.

Do you replace our marketing team or agency?

No — we make them more effective. We set direction, define KPIs and manage the team or agencies you already have, and fill gaps where needed.

Which tools and platforms do you work with?

We're tool-agnostic. We work with your existing stack — CRM, marketing automation, analytics and BI — and recommend changes only when they unlock measurable impact.

How do you measure success?

Against targets agreed during the audit: pipeline, conversion rates, CAC, retention and revenue contribution — not impressions or likes.
